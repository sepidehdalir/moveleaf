Support / iPhone + iPad

A room record,
step by step.

Start and record

  1. Start a room record. Enter a title, move-in/move-out label and your report date. Property details and recorder name are optional.
  2. Add a room. Its six coverage areas begin unmarked. Open each area and choose your own status; add a factual note and photo copies where useful.
  3. Use Add photo to choose a JPEG, HEIC or PNG still from Photos or Files. To take a new photo, use Camera first. Imported copies save immediately; Save applies the area's name, status and note edits.
  4. Review coverage. Condition noted needs a note or photo. Not checked and unmarked areas remain visible as gaps.

Export, save and restore

  1. Choose Create PDF + photo backup from the report overview. Review the PDF in the app.
  2. Use Save or share PDF and Save the photo backup ZIP. Choose Save to Files in the share sheet to keep copies in your chosen folder.
  3. Keep the ZIP as well as the PDF. It includes the photo bytes supplied by your picker and the data needed to restore your report. Those files may contain embedded metadata, including location.
  4. To check a saved ZIP, open Reports > Restore a Moveleaf ZIP backup and choose it from Files. Restore checks its supported structure and photo files before adding a new report. It never overwrites an existing report.

Free supports one active report with up to three rooms. Move the current report to Trash before restoring another free report. Pro supports up to ten active reports and twenty rooms per report. Edited, recompressed, unsupported or damaged ZIPs may be rejected; keep the original backup.

Reports, Trash and templates

Reports shows your local library. Move a report to recoverable Trash to free an active slot; restore it when a slot is available. Permanently deleting a report removes its owned photo copies and cannot be undone. Long-press a room in the overview to move it earlier, save a Pro room template or remove that room after confirmation. Templates copy names, not observations, notes or photos.

Photo and storage limits

Both tiers: 120 photos per report, 25 MB per photo, 60 megapixels and 12,000 pixels per side, 750 MB of photo files per report, and 2 GB across active reports and Trash. Notes support 2,000 characters and 40 lines; captions support 300 characters. Unsupported media is rejected while saved reports are preserved. If storage is full, keep an exported backup and remove unneeded reports from Trash.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro is optional and non-consumable. Open the Pro sheet for Apple's localized price and Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that purchased the app's upgrade. Pending purchases unlock after Apple confirms them. If the App Store is unavailable, try again later; your existing reports remain readable and exportable.

A report or backup will not open

Do not delete the app. Keep your original ZIP and any exported PDF. If a report is listed as unreadable, its files are preserved; avoid permanently deleting it if you still need those files. For help, email the app version, device/OS version, the action you took and the exact error message. Do not send private property photos or a full report by default.
