Privacy / Last updated October 27, 2026

Your room record stays with you.

Moveleaf is developed by Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir (Arvinify). This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app and this support website.

Information in the app

Your report titles, entered property details and report dates, names, room observations, notes, captions and imported photo copies are processed and stored on your device. We do not receive these contents. Moveleaf has no account, developer backend, tracking, advertising or analytics-upload service.

Photos and Files

You choose still images through the system Photos picker or Files picker. The app does not request full photo-library read access, camera, microphone or location permission. Moveleaf keeps the bytes supplied by the picker; those file copies may contain embedded metadata, including location or camera timestamps. An entered report date is not a verified capture date.

Export and sharing

The PDF contains display previews of photos. The ZIP backup contains supplied photo file copies, report data, captions and the PDF. It is not encrypted by Moveleaf. Review the contents and metadata, choose your recipient, and use the share sheet to save or share. Your chosen destination then processes those files under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

Device storage and backups

Reports are stored in the app's local library. Reports moved to Trash remain until you permanently delete them. A linked handover record keeps a locked baseline and a separate current revision in the same local report. Removing a room or owned photo copy from the current revision after confirmation does not remove retained baseline rooms, notes or photo copies. The locked baseline remains until you permanently delete the whole report from Trash; linked PDF and ZIP exports include the retained baseline and current revision. None of these actions changes the source you selected from Photos or Files. Temporary export and picker files are cleared at the next app launch. Apple-managed device backups may include the local library depending on your settings. Moveleaf has no app cloud-sync service. Keep your own exported ZIP backup before deleting the app or changing devices. Copies already exported remain in their destinations until you delete them there.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Lifetime Pro purchase. Moveleaf uses StoreKit to check verified purchase entitlement and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment card details, and the app does not upload purchase records to a developer server. Apple's purchase services follow Apple's privacy policy.

Support and website

If you choose to email hello@arvinify.com, we receive the information you send and use it to respond. Please avoid sending private property photos, full reports or sensitive personal details unless necessary. This static support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics script, advertising pixel or contact form. GitHub may process ordinary hosting request information under its own privacy statement; external links open services with their own policies.

Children

Moveleaf is a general-purpose personal record tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when practices change. Contact hello@arvinify.com with privacy questions. Apple privacy policy · GitHub privacy statement.
